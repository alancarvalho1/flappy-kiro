Instructions for Manual Testing

To manually test the visual effects in-game:

  1. Open the main game: Flappy Kiro
  2. Jump Effect: Press SPACE to see orange particles falling from the ghost
  3. Gap Pass Effect: Pass through a gap to see light blue particles burst
  4. Collision Effect: Collide with a pipe to see red explosion particles
